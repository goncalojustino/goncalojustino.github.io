About

Karina Shimizu

Physical chemist, computational scientist, and educator working at the interface of molecular theory and sustainable chemical engineering.

Karina Shimizu is an Assistant Professor at Instituto Superior Técnico, Universidade de Lisboa, and an integrated researcher at the Centro de Química Estrutural.

Her work focuses on molecular thermodynamics, ionic liquids, and molecular dynamics simulations. She develops computational approaches that reveal how molecular structure controls the behaviour of complex fluids, interfaces, and concentrated solutions, helping transform fundamental insight into materials for energy, carbon management, and greener processes.

Born and educated in Brazil, with Japanese ancestry, she brings an international perspective to a research practice shaped by precision, curiosity, and collaboration. She completed her PhD in Physical Chemistry at the University of São Paulo and has more than two decades of experience across quantum chemistry, force-field development, and molecular simulation.
