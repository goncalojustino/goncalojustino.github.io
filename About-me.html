Gonçalo C. Justino

Gonçalo Justino is a Professor at ESTSetúbal – Universidade Politécnica de Setúbal and a researcher at Centro de Química Estrutural - Institute of Molecular Sciences (IST-UL). His research integrates biochemistry, mass spectrometry, multi-omics, bioinformatics, and computational biology to investigate the molecular mechanisms underlying health, disease, and drug action. His work focuses on biomarker discovery, molecular diagnostics, systems biology, antimicrobial resistance, and translational biomedical research, combining experimental and computational approaches to advance precision medicine and healthcare innovation.
