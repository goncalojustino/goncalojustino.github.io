Research

Complex fluids, made legible.

Research that links molecular structure, dynamics, and thermodynamics to the discovery of useful chemical systems.

01

Ionic liquids & molecular organisation

How do ions assemble, segregate, and reorganise across different environments? Molecular simulations are used to establish the relationships between charge distribution, local structure, and macroscopic properties.

02

Interfaces, nanostructure & solvation

Research examines ionic liquids at solid–liquid interfaces, in mixtures, and in nanostructured media, revealing the molecular interactions that control transport, reactivity, and stability.

03

Energy storage & ion transport

From highly concentrated electrolytes to state-independent ionic conductivity, this work explores how molecular design can produce safer and more adaptable materials for energy applications.

04

Carbon capture & sustainable processes

Task-specific ionic liquids and predictive models support the design of materials for CO₂ capture, conversion, and separation, connecting molecular-level properties to climate-relevant technologies.

05

Predictive & interpretable modelling

Machine learning, molecular descriptors, and computational chemistry are combined to make property prediction faster, more transparent, and more useful for experimental design.
