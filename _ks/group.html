Research group

Building molecular insight together.

A developing group within Molecular Thermodynamics & Engineering at CQE, connecting computation, experiments, and sustainable chemical design.

Group direction

The group works across molecular simulation, physical chemistry, and data-informed modelling to understand and design complex fluids, interfaces, and functional materials.

Current environment

Research is embedded in the Molecular Thermodynamics & Engineering community at the Centro de Química Estrutural, with interdisciplinary collaborations across IST and international partners.

For students

Potential projects span molecular dynamics, quantum chemistry, machine learning for chemical properties, ionic liquids, CO₂ capture, and energy-storage materials.

Supervision

Karina currently supervises and co-supervises projects in plasma catalysis, task-specific ionic liquids, greenhouse-gas solubility, and energy-related molecular dynamics.
